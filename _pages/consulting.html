Teaching: CAS LLMs and AI Agents at HSLU

I teach in the CAS LLMs and AI Agents, a Certificate of Advanced Studies (15 ECTS) at the Lucerne University of Applied Sciences and Arts (HSLU) on building production-ready LLM systems. I have taught the last two editions, covering the full arc from the foundations of LLMs to multi-agent systems.

The program is aimed at professionals and managers in product, data, innovation and digital transformation roles. I use the same practical, production-focused material in my corporate training.

Program modules

  1. Foundations of LLMs tokenization, embeddings, transformers, prompting, reliable LLM apps
  2. Knowledge systems & RAG retrieval pipelines, retrieval quality, verifiable answers
  3. AI agents & multi-agent systems tools, MCP, orchestration, multi-agent workflows, approvals
  4. Quality, safety & responsible use evaluation, red-teaming, guardrails
  5. Customizing & deploying models fine-tuning, smaller models, cloud vs. on-prem
  6. LLMOps & production operations versioning, monitoring, cost control, continuous improvement
